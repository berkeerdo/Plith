# Plith

**The notch for people who care about sound and play games.**

A dynamic island at the top of a Windows screen that replaces the native volume and brightness
flyouts. It knows Voicemeeter, the real output device, what is playing, and it keeps working while
a game runs fullscreen.

## What it is
- Few pages, each excellent: audio, media, shelf, system.
- Motion that feels physical: springs that keep their velocity when interrupted, 240 fps on a
  240 Hz screen, nothing drawn at rest.
- Only Plith: Voicemeeter buses and strips with meters and mute, endpoint pinning, an OSD over
  exclusive fullscreen games, native flyout suppression.

## What it is not
- A toolbox. No prayer times, stocks, mascots or add-on tabs.
- A theme engine. One visual language, done well; a few fixed themes change only its colours
  (ADR 0007).
- Cross-platform. Windows 11 only.

## Who it is for
Windows users with a headset and a mixer (often Voicemeeter), who game and listen while they work,
and who notice when an animation stutters.
