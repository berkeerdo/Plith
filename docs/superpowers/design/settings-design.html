The preview is the point. Every setting on this page changes something you cannot see from the words alone, and the old dialog made you close it, press a volume key, and open it again to find out.

Press Classic or Notch above, or drag the resting height — the preview follows.

This is the fallback, not a bug. It is what a person sees the first time they pick Notch and launch a game — which is why the page says so above rather than leaving them to guess.

Structure

Grouped by what you came for

The old page was one long scroll in config-file order. This splits into what the overlay does and what the app does — and a person adjusting how the notch looks never has to scroll past autostart and hotkeys to reach it.

Dependency

Nesting says what depends on what

Notch-only settings sit indented under Style with a rail beside them, rather than appearing and vanishing as you toggle. Controls that disappear teach people the page is unpredictable; controls that are visibly subordinate teach them the rule.

Copy

Named by what happens

On screen for, not ShowDurationMs. Colour the level, not UseColorThresholds. Each subtitle says what changes, and where a setting has a threshold worth knowing — 85 %, 50 % — it says the number instead of hiding it.

Restraint

No save button

Changes apply as you make them, which the app already does. A save button on a settings page that autosaves is a lie, and one that does not autosave is a trap.
